How it works

From daydream to trip plan in three steps.

Everything you need to explore an idea is together on the Flights page. No account or checkout needed.

01

Set your trip

Choose one way, return, or multi-city. Add your destinations and dates, then select up to four travelers.
02

Compare your options

Press “Search flights” to see sample Kenya Airways and Jambojet-with-partners options, including times, stops, and estimated fares.
03

Preview the cost

Choose a flight to see your route, dates, traveler count, and estimated total. Change your trip details anytime and compare again.
